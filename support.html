About the app

Dart Scoreboard 501 Cricket is a darts scoring app for X01 and Cricket matches, checkout suggestions, training modes, and match history.

What stays free?

Scoring, training, the caller, match statistics, and the full match history remain free. The features from version 1.0 do not require Pro.

What does Pro add?

In version 1.1, Pro adds reports for 7 days, 30 days, or all saved history, plus CSV export. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The App Store shows the price before you confirm.

Reports combine every player in the selected saved matches. They do not track a person's progress across matches or identify players by name. The 7-day and 30-day periods include today, and X01 averages are weighted by the number of darts recorded.

Restore a Pro purchase

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. On the Pro purchase screen, select Restore Purchases. An internet connection may be needed. Restoring Pro access does not restore match history from another device.

If the purchase is still unavailable, follow Apple's purchase restoration guidance or contact us. For a refund request, see Apple's refund instructions.

Export match history

With Pro active, open Reports, choose a period, and select Export CSV. Choose a location in the file picker to save the file. The export contains one row per player in each selected match, including player names and recorded statistics. X01 and Cricket use different statistics, so fields that do not apply are blank.

A CSV is a separate copy for viewing in a spreadsheet or sharing. It is not an app backup that can be imported to restore your matches. Share exported player names and results only with people you intend to receive them.

Offline use and saved history

Scoring and saved match history work offline. The app stores history on the current device and does not provide automatic history synchronization between devices. Removing the app can remove its local history; restoring a Pro purchase restores access to Pro features, not those records.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Dart Scoreboard 501 Cricket helps players score darts matches, review match history, use training modes, and unlock optional Pro features.

Data We Collect

The app may collect anonymous product analytics, including app opens, onboarding progress, match and training events, paywall views, review prompt events, and purchase success/failure events. These events may include a random app-generated device identifier, a random session identifier, app version, game type, number of players, scores, and similar usage details.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers for analytics. Analytics data is not used for tracking and is not linked to your identity.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. The app may record anonymous purchase-related events, such as the product identifier and whether a purchase flow succeeded, to understand feature adoption and fix purchase issues.

Storage

Your match history, training history, settings, and Pro entitlement state are stored on your device. Anonymous analytics events may be queued locally if the device is offline and sent later.

Third-Party Services

The app may send anonymous analytics events to Supabase, which stores the data for product analytics and reliability monitoring. Apple processes in-app purchases and App Store review prompts.

Your Choices

You can delete the app to remove local data stored by the app from your device. To ask privacy questions or request deletion of server-side anonymous analytics associated with details you can provide, contact us at ikorkod@gmail.com.
